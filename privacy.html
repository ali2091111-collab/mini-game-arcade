Privacy Policy

This page describes only what this site actually does today. If the site changes — for example, if advertising, analytics or a contact service is added later — this page must be updated before that change goes live.

What this site is

Mini Game Arcade is a static website made of HTML, CSS and JavaScript files. It has no backend server code, no database and no user accounts.

What the site itself collects

  • Nothing that identifies you. The site does not ask you to sign in, and it does not collect your name, email address or payment details.
  • Game scores are session-only. Your Slice Rush score, timer, lives and combo exist only in the game page while you play. Closing or refreshing the page clears them. Scores are not uploaded, saved to a leaderboard or stored on a server.
  • No cookies or local storage are set by this site's own code. The game and the pages do not use cookies, localStorage or similar browser storage.
  • No advertising or analytics are used. There are no ad scripts, no tracking scripts and no third-party content loaded by these pages. All files are local to the site.
  • Sound is generated in your browser with Web Audio only after you press a button, and can be muted at any time. No audio is recorded or sent anywhere.

Contact form

The form on the Contact page is a placeholder. It is not connected to any email or form service, so anything typed into it stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere. Do not enter personal or payment details into it until the site owner connects a real contact method and updates this policy.

Hosting

When this site is hosted, the hosting provider may keep its own routine server logs (for example, to deliver the pages and keep the service running). Those logs are governed by the hosting provider's own policy, not by this site's code, and this site does not receive or use them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Because the site collects nothing itself, there is no mailing list or account through which to notify you individually.
