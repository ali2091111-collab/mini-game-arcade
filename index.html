Slice Rush

Slice fruit as it flies across the screen, build combos for bonus points, and avoid the bombs. You have 60 seconds and 3 lives.

Controls: mouse drag or touch swipe · Keyboard: Space, R, M

How Slice Rush works

  • Score: each fruit is worth 10 points.
  • Combo: slicing fruit quickly, one after another, adds a bonus — the 2nd in a quick chain adds +5, the 3rd adds +10, and so on.
  • Bombs: do not slice a bomb. A bomb costs one life. You have 3 lives.
  • Timer: the game lasts 60 seconds, or ends early if you lose all 3 lives.
  • Sound: effects are generated in your browser with Web Audio after you press a button, and can be muted at any time.

Your score is kept only on the game page while you play. There is no account, no leaderboard and nothing is uploaded to a server.
