How to play

  • Drag your mouse, finger or stylus across a fruit to slice it.
  • Each fruit is worth 10 points. Slice fruit quickly, one after another, to build a combo bonus.
  • Do NOT slice a bomb. A bomb costs one life. You have 3 lives.
  • The game lasts 60 seconds. Get the highest score you can before time runs out.

Keyboard: Space start / pause   R restart   M sound on / off

About this game

Slice Rush uses only original, simple vector-style shapes drawn in code — no copied artwork, branding or sound files. All sound effects are generated in your browser with Web Audio after you press a button, and you can mute them at any time. Your score is kept only on this page; nothing is uploaded or stored on a server.

Controls

Mouse: hold and drag across fruit. Touch: swipe across fruit. Keyboard: Space start / pause, R restart, M sound on / off. Swiping is confined to the canvas, so normal page scrolling elsewhere still works.
